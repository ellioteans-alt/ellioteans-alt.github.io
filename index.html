Reachwell

Reachwell is a small research and drafting tool for solo consultants and small service businesses. It finds businesses that may need the consultant's service, using public information, and drafts a short, personal first email to each one. It is used privately by its developer for a small number of clients.

How it works

  1. Reachwell looks for prospects (today: independent restaurants in the United States) using public sources: the business's own website and official public records.
  2. It writes a draft email with one specific fact from the business's own website, and records where that fact came from.
  3. With the client's permission, it places each draft in the Drafts folder of the client's own Gmail.
  4. The client reads every draft in Gmail and decides whether to send it. Reachwell never sends email.

Sign in with Google

The client connects his Gmail account with Google's sign-in so that Reachwell can create drafts there. Reachwell asks only for permission to manage drafts (gmail.compose). It uses that permission to create drafts, to check the drafts it created, and to show which account is connected. It does not read the inbox and it does not send mail. Details are in the privacy policy.

For people who receive an email

Every email drafted with Reachwell says who it is from and includes a simple way to opt out: reply to the email and ask not to be contacted again. That address is then never emailed again.

Terms of service

Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. The service

Reachwell researches prospects from public information and drafts personal outreach emails for its clients. With the client's permission it places drafts in the client's own Gmail Drafts folder. Reachwell never sends email.

2. The client decides

The client reviews every draft and decides whether to send it, edit it or delete it. Sending a draft is the client's own action from his own email account.

3. Google account access

The client grants access through Google's sign-in and can remove it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. How that access is used is described in the privacy policy.

4. No guarantees

Reachwell is provided as is. Its research and drafts are prepared with care, but no particular result, reply or business outcome is promised.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information Reachwell uses, why, and what choices you have. Reachwell is operated privately by its developer for a small number of clients. Questions: ellioteans@gmail.com.

1. Google account data (clients who connect Gmail)

A client connects his own Gmail account so that Reachwell can put email drafts in his Drafts folder. Reachwell requests one Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.compose (manage drafts).

Reachwell uses this permission only to:

  • create a draft email in the client's Drafts folder;
  • read back details of a draft it created (the "From" line Gmail saved, and whether the draft still exists), so the client and the operator can confirm the draft is correct or has been sent or deleted;
  • read the email address of the connected account, to show which account is connected.

Reachwell does not read, search or delete messages in the inbox, does not send email, and does not use any other part of the Google account. These are the only Gmail requests the software makes.

2. What Reachwell stores

  • The Google sign-in token, kept on the operator's own computer, outside any shared storage. It is never shared.
  • For each draft it created: the Gmail draft and message ID, the "From" line Gmail saved, the time, and a fingerprint (hash) of the draft text. This is a log of what was put in the client's Drafts folder.

Reachwell does not store the content of any other email in the client's Gmail.

3. How Google data is used and shared

Google account data is used only to provide the drafting feature described above, for the client who connected the account. It is not sold, not shared with anyone else, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Reachwell's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Information about businesses Reachwell contacts

To find prospects, Reachwell uses public information only: the business's own website (home, about, contact, location and menu pages, while respecting each site's robots.txt) and official public records. It uses business contact details only, never personal email addresses, and never guesses email addresses. Google Places is used only to find businesses and their websites; Reachwell keeps only Google's place ID for each business.

If you received an email drafted with Reachwell and do not want further contact, reply and say so in any words. Your address is added to a do-not-contact list and is never emailed again. That list is kept so the request can be honored.

5. Your choices

  • A client can disconnect Reachwell at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The operator then deletes the stored token.
  • Anyone can ask what information Reachwell holds about them, or ask for it to be deleted, by writing to ellioteans@gmail.com. A do-not-contact entry is kept only to keep honoring an opt-out.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
